A Reproducibility Checklist for Machine Learning Systems Papers

Abstract. We present a checklist for machine learning systems papers and apply it to 60
submissions. The most frequently missing item is a description of the compute
environment, which appears in 11 percent of accepted papers.

1. Introduction

Reproducibility claims are usually unfalsifiable because the artifacts they name are
not specified. We reduce the checklist to eleven items and argue that six of them are
necessary and sufficient.

2. The checklist

An item is necessary when omitting it makes a stated claim untestable. Compute
environment, data version, random seed handling, preprocessing pipeline, stopping
criterion and evaluation protocol meet this bar.

3. Application

We applied the checklist to 60 accepted papers at three venues. Median completeness was
seven of eleven. The items most often marked present were the least often actually
sufficient.

4. Limitations

We scored presence, not correctness. A paper that states a compute environment it did
not use is scored as present by our method.
