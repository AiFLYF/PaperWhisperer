Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Long Technical Documents

Abstract. We describe a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline for summarising long
technical documents. The system indexes document elements by embedding and answers
queries against a hybrid lexical and dense retriever. We report retrieval recall and
citation precision on an annotated subset of 52 documents.

1. Introduction

Long technical documents exceed the context window of current language models, so a
summary must be assembled from retrieved passages rather than from the whole file. Our
pipeline therefore separates retrieval from generation: passages are selected first and
the model is shown only what was selected.

2. Method

We chunk each document by structural element, embed every chunk, and store the vectors
alongside the element locator. At query time we combine BM25 scores with cosine
similarity and re-rank the union. Generation is conditioned on the top-k passages.

3. Results

Retrieval recall@10 is 0.91 on our annotated subset, and 0.84 on tables, which we
attribute to the fact that a flattened table loses the column a value belongs to.

4. Limitations

Our evaluation covers English and Chinese technical reports. We do not evaluate
equation-heavy manuscripts, where chunk boundaries frequently split a formula.
