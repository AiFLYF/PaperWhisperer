Decoding temperature contributes the largest share of run-to-run variance, followed by random restarts. Prompt formatting effects are smaller but statistically significant across all benchmarks. We recommend reporting i
nterval estimates rather than single-point scores.
3. Recommendations
Our protocol reduces reported variance by 48 percent relative to common practice. We release all prompts, seeds, and parsing code to make single-number leaderboards directly reproducible.
