A Survey of Evidence-Grounded Document Analysis
<b>English body with Chinese section summaries.</b> This sample exercises parsers and retrievers on documents where the primary language alternates between sections.
1. Introduction
Evidence-grounded analysis requires that every claim in a generated answer can be traced back to a concrete location in the source document. Early summarisation systems flattened documents into plain text and therefore lost page structure, element types and provenance.
本节要点：证据驱动的分析要求每条结论都能回溯到原文的具体位置，早期的摘要系统把文档压平为纯文本，丢失了版面结构、元素类型与来源信息。
2. Parsing and Provenance
Modern parser gateways expose a canonical document model with elements, tables, figures, formulas and source locators. The locator schema differs per format: PDF pages with bounding boxes, slides for decks, element references for word processor files.
本节要点：现代解析网关提供统一的文档模型，包含元素、表格、图片、公式与定位符。定位语义按格式而异：PDF 用页码与包围盒，演示文稿用幻灯片，文字处理文档用元素引用。
3. Retrieval Strategies
Hybrid retrieval combines dense semantic recall with lexical matching. For mixed Chinese and English corpora, lexical matching on Chinese text needs substring or trigram strategies rather than whitespace tokenisation alone.
本节要点：混合检索结合稠密语义召回与词法匹配。对中英文混合语料，中文部分的词法匹配需要子串或 trigram 策略，不能只依赖空格分词。
4. Verification
Direct quotations must be verified programmatically against the canonical source text. Quotes that fail exact or normalised alignment are downgraded to paraphrase and must not be rendered with quotation marks.
本节要点：直接引文必须以程序方式针对规范原文进行校验，无法逐字对齐的内容应降级为改写，且不得以引号形式呈现。
5. Open Problems
Remaining problems include cross-page table recovery, reading order in three-column layouts, and cost control for on-demand vision models.
本节要点：尚未解决的问题包括跨页表格还原、三栏版式的阅读顺序恢复，以及按需视觉模型的成本控制。
