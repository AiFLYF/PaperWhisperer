Functional Integral Representations
in Quantum Field Theory
We review the construction of functional integrals for scalar and gauge fields and derive the one-loop effective action by the background field method. Explicit computations are carried out for the O(N) model in two and
 four dimensions.
1. The Generating Functional
Z[J] = integral D phi exp(i S[phi] + i integral J phi)
S[phi] = integral d^4x (1/2 partial phi partial phi - 1/2 m^2 phi^2 - lambda/4! phi^4)
The generating functional Z[J] formally integrates over all field configurations weighted by the exponential of the classical action. Perturbation theory arises from expanding around the stationary point of the action funct
ional.
2. Background Field Method
Gamma[phi_B] = -i ln Z[J] - integral J phi_B
d = 4 - epsilon,  lambda_R = lambda mu^epsilon Z_lambda
Splitting the field into a classical background and a quantum fluctuation preserves gauge invariance of the effective action order by order. The divergent part of the one-loop determinant yields the beta function of the qua
rtic coupling.
3. Renormalization
beta(lambda) = 3 lambda^2 / (16 pi^2) + O(lambda^3)
The one-loop beta function shows the theory is not asymptotically free for positive lambda. We compare with exact results available in two dimensions and discuss the resummation of large logarithms.
